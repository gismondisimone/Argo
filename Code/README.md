> second prototype of the archeo-helper Argo, from Bitetto to Zagabria!

## ✶ Robot parts:
- **Rover module** - Movement unit, equipped with a map-remembering algorithm that follows you around and automatically finds it way back to the base when requested.
- **Buffer module** - Long and efficient sectionated conveyor belt that gets filled on site with artifacts and keeps them safe until scan.
- **scanner module** - The heart of the robot, where the phisical artefacts get scanned and become digitally accessibile.
- **Archeo-site** - Online archive where to publish all the scans with descriptions and make them accessibile to all archeologists around the world.
